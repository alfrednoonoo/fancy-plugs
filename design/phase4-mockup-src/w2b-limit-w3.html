Fancy Limit: v1.7.2 layout mockup (multiband limiting, ▲)

docs/enhancements.md, section 10 item 6: 2 to 4 bands with a release each. It changes the visible layout, so the layout comes before the design note. Changed things in dashed yellow, on the plugin's real screenshot (1x coordinates). The parameters drawn here (band count, crossovers, a release per band) are proposals the note will set out as decisions.
1 · A (recommended): a BANDS card between the display and the LIMITER card; the window grows 172 px taller (760 x 637)
Bands 1 to 4 (1 by default, today's limiter), the Crossover type (MultiComp's list), and a strip with each band's reduction falling from the top in its colour. Drag a blue handle to move a crossover, or click its frequency to type one. Under the strip, each band has its own small Release knob and GR read-out. The bands are limited first and summed; the LIMITER card's Ceiling, Release, Lookahead and True peak then hold the final ceiling across the whole signal, as now.

1 · Footer: "Listen to removed" (Decision 3 A)
The only change to the main layout. A fifth footer checkbox, a temporary listen switch like Preview filter (§0.3: not a parameter, off when the window closes, never saved). The two are exclusive: switching one on switches the other off. The five are spaced evenly across the 750 px left of the Out meter, each placed by its measured width (positions below). Top: today. Bottom: with the new switch on, in the accent, as Limit's and Sature's listen switches are.
2 · EDIT drawer, Options page: Hold and Range
Today's Options page with two knobs added under Link amount and Output, the same medium knob. Hold 0 to 500 ms, default 0 (today's sound): the release waits this long after the last peak. Range 1 to 40 dB with "Off" at the top, default Off: the most reduction Comp will apply, so heavy settings ride the level without crushing it. Both dim nothing else; nothing else on the page moves.

3 · EDIT drawer, History page (Decision 4 A, with the user's additions of 21 November 2026)
A fifth page in the view switch, oldest on the left, one column per pixel. The input waveform in grey and the output waveform in teal over it, so the gap between them is what Comp took off (before make-up, so the two line up when nothing is compressed). The gain reduction is the yellow line from the top (0 to 24 dB, right scale), the GR meter's colour; unlinked, the right channel is a lighter second line. The effective threshold is a dashed pair of lines at ± its level; it moves with Auto threshold. Zoom: 1.5, 3, 6 or 12 s. Beat and bar lines from the host's tempo while it plays (bars brighter), switched by "Grid". Pause: click the graph to freeze it, and again to go on. The read-out is the largest reduction in the shown span; click it to clear. None of these are parameters; zoom and Grid are remembered per editor, like the drawer's page.

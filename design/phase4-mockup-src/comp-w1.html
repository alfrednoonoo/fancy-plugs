1 · Footer: "Listen to removed" (Decision 3 A)
The only change to the main layout. A fifth footer checkbox, a temporary listen switch like Preview filter (§0.3: not a parameter, off when the window closes, never saved). The two are exclusive: switching one on switches the other off. The five are spaced evenly across the 750 px left of the Out meter, each placed by its measured width (positions below). Top: today. Bottom: with the new switch on, in the accent, as Limit's and Sature's listen switches are.
2 · EDIT drawer, Options page: Hold and Range
Today's Options page with two knobs added under Link amount and Output, the same medium knob. Hold 0 to 500 ms, default 0 (today's sound): the release waits this long after the last peak. Range 1 to 40 dB with "Off" at the top, default Off: the most reduction Comp will apply, so heavy settings ride the level without crushing it. Both dim nothing else; nothing else on the page moves.

3 · EDIT drawer, History page (Decision 4 A)
A fifth page in the view switch. The last 3 seconds, oldest on the left, one column per pixel (about 6.5 ms each): the detector level as a grey fill (0 to −60 dB, left scale), the effective threshold as a white line (it moves with Auto threshold), and the gain reduction in yellow from the top (0 to 30 dB, right scale), the GR meter's colour. Unlinked, the left channel is the solid yellow and the right a lighter line over it; linked, the two are one. The read-out at the top right is the largest reduction in the shown 3 s; click it to clear. While the drawer is open, the threshold meter, GR meter and faders stay visible to its left and right.

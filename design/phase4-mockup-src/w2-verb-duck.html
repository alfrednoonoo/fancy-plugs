The SETUP drawer: an AFTER THE REVERB group
In the drawer's free space beside the existing settings: the detector's Source (Main or Sidechain), Ducking (0 to 100 %, 0 by default) and the Gate (off by default; Threshold −60 to 0 dB, Hold 10 to 500 ms, Decay 5 to 500 ms). Not modules: they act live on the wet signal after the convolver, whatever the rack's order.

Ducking 0 and Gate off (the defaults) leave today's sound to the bit. The detector waits for the reported latency, so in Low CPU mode it still lines up with the wet. Gate's three knobs dim while Gate is off. With no sidechain routed, Sidechain acts as Main.

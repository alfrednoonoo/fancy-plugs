docs/delay.md, Phase 4 Wave 3: reverse and pitch-shifted taps, Decisions 1 to 4 A (user, 26 November 2026). New things in dashed yellow, on the plugin's real screenshot (1x coordinates).
1 · The Tone page, tap 1 as it loads: forward, 0 st. The window grows 64 px wider, to 884 x 472
Pitch (−12 to +12 st, 0 by default, centred) is a seventh knob after Pan, at the six knobs' own 64 px spacing: none of them moves. To give it its column, the window grows 64 px wider, from 820 to 884 px (its minimum too): the tap graph and the knob card widen, and Volume, the meters and the faders move across, as in Wave 2. Reverse is a two-way direction switch, ▶ | ◀, under Feedback, where the wave switch sits under Rate on the Motion page.

◀︎
Forward and 0 st are today's sound, to the bit. Both are per tap, like Feedback and Pan. The graph keeps 0 to 2000 ms across its new width.
2 · Tap 1 reversed, +7 st
With ◀ chosen the tap plays each window of one tap time backwards, so a note swells in and ends on its attack. Its tab gets a small ◀ in its bottom-right corner, so a reversed tap shows on every page.
▶︎
◀︎
◀
Pitch shifts each repeat again, inside the feedback loop: +12 st gives rising octaves, −12 falling ones, ±0.1 st a slow drift. Typed values take 7, -12 or -0.1. A tooltip on the switch reads "Forward" or "Reverse: plays each window of one tap time backwards".
3 · The Motion page of the same tap, in the wider window
Rate, Depth, Random, Stereo and Diffusion spread to the same 64 px spacing as the Tone page (today 56 to 63 px), using the width the window gains; the wave switch stays under Rate. The Character letter keeps the tab's top-right corner and the ◀ takes the bottom-right one, so a Tape tap that's reversed shows both.
◀
Only the positions change; no knob, label or switch changes size.

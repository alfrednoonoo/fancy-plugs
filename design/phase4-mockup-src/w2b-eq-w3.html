1 · The band editor, band 2 dynamic: SPECTRAL off, as it loads
SPECTRAL (per band, off by default) sits under ST and DYN, spanning both (94 x 24, the DYN chip's height), in the column's empty space: no control moves or changes size, and the window stays 802 x 645. It shows only while DYN is on, like Threshold to Release.
SPECTRAL
Off is today's dynamic band, to the bit, with no latency. Tooltip: "Spectral: works per frequency inside the band, so a resonance is cut on its own and the rest of the band is left alone. Adds 21 ms of latency."
2 · SPECTRAL on
The chip lights in the band's colour. The band's Threshold, Range, Attack, Release, Above | Below, Sidechain and Reach act per frequency. On the graph the white total curve shows a narrow dip at whatever is being cut, instead of the whole bell moving; "now" reads the deepest point.
SPECTRAL
The first spectral band adds 1024 samples of latency (21 ms at 48 kHz), reported to the host; the last one off takes it away. Threshold is the level at one frequency, so set it by the resonance's level.
3 · Linear phase: the chip is disabled
Spectral needs Minimum phase, as DYN does; in Linear mode the chip greys out and spectral bands play their static settings.

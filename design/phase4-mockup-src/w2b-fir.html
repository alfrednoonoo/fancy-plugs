1 · Compressor mode, editing the threshold curve
A Threshold | Ratio switch at the graph's top left picks which curve the points edit; the other stays drawn, dimmed and dashed. With Mid/Side on, a Mid | Side switch sits beside the Mode row. To fit it at full size, the window grows 70 px wider, from 760 to 830 px (its minimum too): the graph gets wider rather than padded, so its frequency axis stays continuous. Compressor mode's cards are redrawn as built, because CI has no Compressor screenshot; the EQ screenshot is underneath.

3 · The EDIT drawer: Mid/Side
A Mid/Side checkbox (off by default) beside Learn length and Output. Off, FIR is as it was, bit for bit, and the Mid | Side switch isn't shown.
Mid/Side
Every mode works on mid and side instead of left and right, each with its own curve, Learn and gains. The Mid | Side switch beside the Mode row picks which one you edit. Convolve L/R stays left and right.

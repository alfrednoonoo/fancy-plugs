The depth ceiling and its nodes
In Resonance mode the graph draws the most each frequency may be cut (Depth × the nodes × Tilt), hanging from the 0 dB line between Low and High, with the green reduction inside it. Nodes are a multiplier on Depth, 0 to 200 % (Decision 1 A), drawn where they land: here Depth 6 dB with 50 % at 300 Hz, 200 % at 3 kHz (the selected node, with its read-out) and 150 % at 10 kHz. Double-click adds a node, drag moves it, right-click deletes it; the Edit menu's Points (smooth), Points (flat) and Freehand work as on the Gate and Compressor curves. Reset curve, in the Range card's top row, goes back to flat.

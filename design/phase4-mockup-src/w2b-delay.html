Triangle: a steady detune that flips each half cycle
Both are per tap, like Rate and Depth. Sine at 90° is today's sound, to the bit. 0° moves both sides together; 180° moves them in opposition, the widest. The triangle's corners are rounded over 10 % of the cycle, so its flips don't jump.

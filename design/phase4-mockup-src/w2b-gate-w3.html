Fancy Gate: v1.7.2 layout mockup (multiband gating, ▲)

docs/enhancements.md, section 9 item 7: 2 to 3 bands. It changes the visible layout, so the layout comes before the design note. Changed things in dashed yellow, on the plugin's real screenshot (1x coordinates). The parameters drawn here (band count, crossovers, each band's Threshold and Range) are proposals the note will set out as decisions.
1 · A (recommended): a BANDS card between the graph and the envelope card, as Limit's; the window grows 172 px taller (790 x 637)
Bands 1 to 3 (1 by default, today's gate), the Crossover type, and a strip with each band's attenuation falling from the top in its colour: here a drum overhead, the lows open, the mids shut on bleed, the highs part-way. Drag a blue line to move a crossover, or click it to type one. Under the strip each band has its own Threshold (an offset from the Threshold fader, so the fader still moves them all) and Range, and its open or shut state. Attack, Hold, Release, Hysteresis and the detector stay shared, in the cards below.

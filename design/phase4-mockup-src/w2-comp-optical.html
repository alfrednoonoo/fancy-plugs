The Options page: an Optical checkbox
Under "Attack = 90 % rise", with the other ballistics options. Off by default. While it's on, the Classic and Auto release checkboxes on the Detector page dim. No memory knob (Decision 3 A).
Optical
Attack slows as it closes in, release has a fast and a slow stage, and a long squash recovers slowly. Classic and Auto release don't apply while it's on.
Attack then means the time at about 12 dB over, which the tooltip says. Weird knee, Hold and Range still work.

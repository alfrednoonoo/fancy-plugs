Fancy Plugs theme for REAPER: mockup

For docs/reaper-theme.md. 100 % size (REAPER will show it at your 125 %). Drawn from the suite's design values; nothing copied from REAPER's own themes or the Reapertips reference. The track colours, the arrangement and the plugin names are sample content.

Track colour palette: 12 hues in rainbow order, each with a dark shade

* = one of the plugins' accents. Top row: the colours. Bottom row: the dark shades (65 % brightness). The toolbar's rainbow button opens them as a menu.

A. Light tint over grey (recommended)

Track colour as a strip on the panel, a light tint behind it, coloured items, and the colour on the mixer strip's name. Panels and mixer stay grey like the plugins' bodies. Track 3 is selected; Drums is record-armed; Bass is muted; Vox is soloed.

B. Filled

The same window with the track colour filling the panels, items and mixer strips.

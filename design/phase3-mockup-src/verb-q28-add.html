1 · The rack with room to spare: + Add module after the last card
The cards lose their bottom-right +. After the last card sits a dashed box the size of the add panel (203 × 352), with a round + and "Add module". It's always the last thing in the rack: when the chain is wider than the rack, it's past the last card and you scroll to it, as you do to the last card now. An empty rack keeps today's centred buttons and has no box.

2 · After a click: the add panel in its place, with Cancel
The panel replaces the box, at the same size, so nothing moves. The eight types are the existing buttons, 28 high at 34 px spacing (they were 32 at 38) so Cancel fits under them. A type adds its module at the end of the chain and closes the panel; Cancel, Esc or a click elsewhere in the rack closes it without adding. Modulate is greyed while the chain has one. To put a new module earlier in the chain, drag its card by the side grip, as now.

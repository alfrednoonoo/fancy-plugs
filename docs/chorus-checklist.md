# Fancy Chorus: REAPER checklist

Install with `scripts\get-latest.ps1`, then in REAPER:

- [ ] Fancy Chorus appears under VST3 as "Fancy Chorus"; the window matches mockup 51 (Mode, display, MOTION, VOICES, TONE cards, footer, Out/Wet/Dry) in pink.
- [ ] On a guitar or pad at its defaults: a wide, shimmering chorus; the display's three lines move and the dots sit left, centre and right.
- [ ] Each Mode: Classic, Ensemble, Dimension, Doubler, Flange sound distinct; switching while it plays doesn't click, and Voices stays where it was.
- [ ] Turn Rate, Depth, Delay, Voices, Spread and Width while it plays: no clicks; the display follows.
- [ ] Flange, Feedback +60 % and -60 %: jet sweeps; Feedback +90 % stays stable.
- [ ] Width 0: the chorus is mono. Keep bass mono on a bass: the low end stays centred.
- [ ] Tempo sync, 1/4 at the project's tempo: the sweep lands on the beat, and stays locked after seeking.
- [ ] EDIT > Ducking 100 % on a vocal: the chorus dips under the singing and swells in the gaps.
- [ ] Listen to wet plays only the voices, and turns off when the window closes.
- [ ] Every preset loads; Save, reopen: the settings come back.
- [ ] Render offline twice: the renders match.

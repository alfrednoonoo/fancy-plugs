Fancy Plugs for macOS 11 or later (Intel and Apple silicon), VST3 and Audio Unit
================================================================================

Install
-------

1. Close your DAW.
2. Double-click "Install Fancy Plugs.command".
3. The first time, macOS stops it with "Apple could not verify ..." because Fancy Plugs isn't
   signed with an Apple Developer ID. Click Done, then:
   open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
   "Install Fancy Plugs.command was blocked", click Open Anyway and confirm with your password.
   (On macOS 14 or earlier you can instead right-click the file and choose Open.)
4. A Terminal window opens, installs the plugins and says how many it installed.
   Press Enter to close it.
5. Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins.

The VST3s go to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and the Audio Units to
~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components, your own folders, so no administrator password is needed.
Use the VST3s in REAPER, Live, Cubase, Bitwig and Studio One, and the Audio Units in Logic Pro
and GarageBand.

Why the script: macOS won't load an unsigned plugin that came from a download until its
quarantine flag is removed. The script copies the plugins and removes that flag.

Installing by hand instead
--------------------------

Copy the contents of VST3 and Components into the folders above (in Finder: Go menu, hold
Option, Library, then Audio > Plug-Ins). Then open Terminal, paste this line and press Return:

  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Fancy* ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Fancy*

Uninstall
---------

Delete the "Fancy ..." plugins from those two folders.
